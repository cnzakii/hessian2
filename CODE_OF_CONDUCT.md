# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, respectful community for users and contributors of all
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, viewpoints, and boundaries.
- Credit other people's work and respect their privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, unwanted sexual attention,
threats, impersonation, and publishing private information without consent
are not acceptable. These expectations apply in project spaces and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through a contact explicitly published by a
maintainer. Do not publish personal details in an issue. If a report concerns
a maintainer, or no private project contact is available, use the hosting
platform's private reporting mechanism for the relevant content. For GitHub,
see [reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove
someone from project spaces. They will consider context and severity, protect
reporters' privacy, and communicate decisions to the people involved where
appropriate. Retaliation against a good-faith report is itself a violation.

Before opening public community channels, maintainers must publish a working
private contact route. This policy does not promise a response deadline or
confidentiality beyond what the hosting platform and maintainers can provide.
